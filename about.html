Who We Are

ChinaWiFiGo is operated by Wuhan City Zhierchong Culture and Tourism Co., Ltd. (武汉市之二虫文旅有限公司), a licensed travel agency dedicated to providing international visitors with seamless internet access during their travels in China.

Our company was founded in 2015 and renamed Wuhan City Zhierchong Culture & Tourism Co., Ltd. (武汉市之二虫文旅有限公司) in June 2025, when we turned fully to inbound travel. We are headquartered in Wuhan, Hubei Province. Our mission is simple: to remove the connectivity barrier that international visitors face when travelling in China.

Our Story

Every year, millions of international visitors come to China for business and tourism. Yet, accessing reliable internet remains one of the biggest challenges due to language barriers, unfamiliar systems, and registration requirements.

We started ChinaWiFiGo to solve this problem. By providing easy-to-use pocket WiFi devices with unlimited high-speed 4G/5G data, we enable travelers to stay connected with family, friends, and work while exploring China.
