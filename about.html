About ChinaWiFiGo

Who We Are

ChinaWiFiGo is operated by Wuhan City Zhierchong Culture and Tourism Co., Ltd. (武汉市之二虫文旅有限公司), a licensed travel agency dedicated to providing international visitors with seamless internet access during their travels in China.

Founded in 2015 and headquartered in Wuhan, Hubei Province, we have served over 50,000 travelers from around the world. Our mission is simple: to eliminate the connectivity barrier that international visitors face when traveling to China.

Our Story

Every year, millions of international visitors come to China for business and tourism. Yet, accessing reliable internet remains one of the biggest challenges due to language barriers, unfamiliar systems, and registration requirements.

We started ChinaWiFiGo to solve this problem. By providing easy-to-use pocket WiFi devices with built-in VPN, we enable travelers to stay connected to their world while exploring China.
