Name Origin

Hangzhou (杭州) means "Crossing the River". Capital of the Wuyue Kingdom, immortalized by Marco Polo as the finest city in the world.

Happiness Index

7.7/10 — A tech hub with a poetic soul — residents enjoy West Lake mornings, Longjing tea, and Alibaba evenings.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Hangzhou is elegant, green, and slightly smug about its lake. Locals bike around West Lake, drink Longjing tea, and complain about the summer humidity. The city balances tech wealth with classical Chinese aesthetics.

Fun Facts:

• West Lake is a UNESCO World Heritage site and has inspired Chinese poets and painters for over 1,000 years.

• Hangzhou is home to Alibaba, one of the world's largest e-commerce companies, founded by Jack Ma in an apartment here.

• The Grand Canal, which runs through Hangzhou, is the longest artificial river in the world and over 1,700 years old.

Speak Like a Local

Hangzhou locals speak their own flavor of Mandarin. Try these phrases to earn a smile:

伢儿 (yá ér) — Kid / child — a Hangzhou way to refer to young people.

落胃 (là wèi) — Comfortable in the stomach — the perfect description of a satisfying meal.

蛮好 (mán hǎo) — Pretty good / not bad — typical Hangzhou understatement.

Famous People

• Su Shi (Su Dongpo) — The Song dynasty poet and governor built the famous Su Causeway across West Lake.

• Jack Ma — Founder of Alibaba Group, one of China's most influential entrepreneurs, started his company in Hangzhou.

• Yue Fei — The legendary Song dynasty general is buried and honored near West Lake.
