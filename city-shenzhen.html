Area & Population

Area: 1,997 km²
Population: 17.6 million

Name Origin

Shenzhen (深圳) means "Deep Drainage". A small town in 1979, it became China's first Special Economic Zone and is now the Silicon Valley of China.

Happiness Index

7.6/10 — Residents enjoy a fast-paced, innovative lifestyle with beaches, parks, and a thriving tech ecosystem.

Metro & Transport

16 subway lines, 567 km

Universities

Shenzhen University, SUSTech, HIT Shenzhen

Natural Resources

Dapeng Peninsula beaches, Wutong Mountain, Dameisha and Xiaomeisha beaches, Shenzhen Bay Park, Mangrove forests.

Attractions in Shenzhen

Window of the World, Splendid China, Shenzhen Bay Park, Ping An Finance Centre, Dafen Oil Painting Village, OCT Loft, Dameisha Beach.

Popular Routes

Day 1: Window of the World → Splendid China → OCT Loft; Day 2: Shenzhen Bay Park → Ping An Finance Centre observation deck; Day 3: Dameisha Beach → Dapeng Fortress.

Food & Dining

Cantonese seafood, Chaozhou beef hotpot, Hakka cuisine, Shenzhen-style claypot rice, Dim sum, BBQ oysters.

Shopping

Dongmen Pedestrian Street, Coco Park, Huaqiangbei Electronics Market, Sea World, MixC World, Shenzhen Bay MixC.
