Name Origin

Kunming (昆明) means "Eternal Spring and Brightness". Ancient capital of the Dian Kingdom. Known as the City of Eternal Spring for its mild climate year-round.

Happiness Index

7.5/10 — A city of flowers and ethnic diversity — residents enjoy year-round blooms, mild weather, and diverse cultures.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Kunming is the 'City of Eternal Spring' and feels like it. Locals eat mushrooms, barbecue, and rice noodles at all hours, and the weather is so mild that many homes don't have air conditioning. The city is relaxed, multicultural, and a gateway to Yunnan's wild landscapes.

Fun Facts:

• Kunming has an average annual temperature of about 15°C, giving it the nickname 'Spring City.'

• The Stone Forest, a UNESCO site nearby, is a maze of limestone pillars formed over 270 million years.

• Yunnan is one of the most biodiverse provinces in China, home to over 18,000 plant species and many ethnic minority groups.

Speak Like a Local

Kunming locals speak their own flavor of Mandarin. Try these phrases to earn a smile:

板扎 (bǎn zhā) — Great / excellent — the classic Kunming compliment.

买哪样 (mǎi nǎ yàng) — What are you buying? — used like 'What's up?' in some contexts.

克哪点 (kè nǎ diǎn) — Where are you going? — local Kunming Mandarin.

Famous People

• Zheng He — The Ming dynasty explorer and admiral was born in Kunming's region and led seven great maritime expeditions.

• Nie Er — The composer of China's national anthem, March of the Volunteers, was born in Kunming.

• Joseph Rock — An Austrian-American explorer and botanist who lived in Yunnan for decades and introduced its culture to the West.
