Area & Population

Area: 24,061 km² (autonomous prefecture, capital Enshi City)
Population: Approximately 3.5 million (prefecture, 2020 census)

Name Origin

Enshi means "bestowed by the emperor" or "imperial grace" in Chinese. It is a Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture in southwestern Hubei.

Happiness Index

7.0/10 — Residents enjoy a balanced lifestyle with modern amenities and cultural richness.

Metro & Transport

Enshi Xujiaping Airport (ENH) offers domestic flights. Enshi Railway Station connects to Yichang and Chongqing on the Yichang–Wanzhou railway. Buses and taxis serve the prefecture.

Universities

Hubei Minzu University (Enshi campus) and Enshi Vocational and Technical College are the main local institutions.

Natural Resources

Enshi lies in the Wuling Mountains with karst canyons, rivers, and forests. It has a subtropical mountain climate and is famous for selenium-rich soil and tea.

Attractions in Enshi

Visit Enshi Grand Canyon, Tenglong Cave, Tusi City, Qing River, and the Suobuyu Stone Forest.

Popular Routes

Day 1: Visit Enshi Grand Canyon and Tusi City.
Day 2: Explore Tenglong Cave and the Qing River.

Food & Dining

Try Enshi cured pork, Hezha (a fermented soybean dish), Tujia-style grilled fish, potato rice, and selenium-rich tea.

Shopping

Enshi Yu Lu (jade dew) green tea, Tujia embroidery, selenium products, and cured meats are popular. Main shopping is in Enshi City center.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Enshi (恩施) is proud, spicy, and revolutionary. Locals love chili, late nights, and talking politics over grilled fish. Visitors love the authentic atmosphere, friendly advice, and the way Enshi cured pork shows up on every local menu.

Fun Facts:

• Enshi is home to Enshi Grand Canyon, a local highlight that reflects the city's character and history.

• The city blends everyday modern life with long-standing traditions, making it a great place to experience real China.

• Enshi cured pork is one of the local flavors visitors remember most — ask a local where they eat it.

Speak Like a Local

Locals in Enshi have their own regional flavor of Mandarin. Try these to sound like one of the crowd:

恰饭 (qià fàn) — Eat food.

妹陀 (mèi tuó) — Girl.

伢子 (yá zi) — Boy.

Famous People

• Mao Zedong — The founder of the People’s Republic of China was born in Hunan.

• Liu Shaoqi — A major early Chinese leader was also from Hunan.

• Yuan Longping — The father of hybrid rice, associated with Hunan’s rice fields, revolutionized global food security.
