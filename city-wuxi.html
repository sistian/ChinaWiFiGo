Area & Population

Area: 4,627 km²
Population: ~7.5 million (2020)

Name Origin

Wuxi means "without tin" in Chinese, referring to the historical depletion of tin mines. It is located on the Yangtze River Delta in southern Jiangsu and is famous for its industry and Lake Tai.

Happiness Index

7.0/10 — Residents enjoy a balanced lifestyle with modern amenities and cultural richness.

Metro & Transport

Wuxi Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX) has domestic and international flights. Wuxi Railway Station and Wuxi East Station are major rail hubs on the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway. The city has three metro lines (Lines 1, 2, and 3).

Universities

Jiangnan University, Wuxi University, and Wuxi Taihu University are main institutions.

Natural Resources

Wuxi lies on the shores of Lake Tai (Taihu) with a humid subtropical climate. It has lakes, canals, and is known for its beautiful gardens and industry.

Attractions in Wuxi

Visit Lingshan Grand Buddha, Taihu Lake (Lake Tai), Turtle Head Isle (Yuantouzhu), Nanchan Temple, and the Grand Canal.

Popular Routes

Day 1: Taihu Lake and Yuantouzhu.
Day 2: Lingshan Grand Buddha and ancient canal area.

Food & Dining

Wuxi is famous for sweet and savory dishes: Wuxi spare ribs, Taihu whitefish, steamed buns, and local rice cakes.

Shopping

Buy Wuxi clay figurines (Huishan), Taihu tea, silk products, and local snacks. Main shopping is on Zhongshan Road and Nanchan Temple.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Wuxi is a wealthy, lake-loving city on the shores of Taihu. Locals are proud of their sweet spare ribs, their Buddhist giant statue, and the fact that they live well without boasting. It's the Jiangsu city that prefers good food over flashy skyline.

Fun Facts:

• Wuxi is famous for the Grand Buddha at Ling Shan, one of China's largest bronze Buddha statues.

• The city sits on Lake Tai, China's third-largest freshwater lake, and has long been a center of silk and grain trade.

• Wuxi-style spare ribs are sweet, red, and so beloved that locals claim no visitor should leave without trying them.

Speak Like a Local

Wuxi locals speak their own flavor of Mandarin. Try these phrases to earn a smile:

倷好 (nèi hǎo) — Hello — classic Wu dialect greeting.

灵个 (líng gè) — Very good / excellent — Wuxi style.

白相 (bái xiàng) — To play / have fun.

Famous People

• Gu Kaizhi — A famous Eastern Jin dynasty painter from Wuxi, considered one of the founders of Chinese figure painting.

• Xu Beihong — One of China's most celebrated modern painters, born in Yixing near Wuxi, known for his powerful horse paintings.

• Qian Zhongshu — A brilliant 20th-century writer and scholar from Wuxi, best known for the novel Fortress Besieged.
