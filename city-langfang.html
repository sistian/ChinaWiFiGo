Area & Population

Area: 6,417 km²
Population: ~5.5 million (2020)

Name Origin

Langfang means "lang's house" or "official post house" in Chinese. It is located midway between Beijing and Tianjin in Hebei Province.

Happiness Index

7.0/10 — Residents enjoy a balanced lifestyle with modern amenities and cultural richness.

Metro & Transport

Langfang has no commercial airport; Beijing and Tianjin airports are used. Langfang Railway Station and Langfang North Station connect to Beijing and Tianjin on the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Universities

Langfang Normal University and North China Institute of Aerospace Engineering are the main institutions.

Natural Resources

Langfang lies on the North China Plain with a temperate continental climate. It is between the Yongding River and Chaobai River basins.

Attractions in Langfang

Visit the Grand Canal cultural area in Xianghe, the Natural Park, and the ancient post road museum.

Popular Routes

Day 1: Grand Canal cultural area and city museum.
Day 2: Natural Park and local markets.

Food & Dining

Try Langfang baked sesame cake, smoked chicken, Hebei-style noodles, and Beijing-influenced snacks.

Shopping

Grand Canal souvenirs, local snacks, and Hebei crafts are sold in Guangyang District.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Langfang (廊坊) is northern, practical, and surrounds Beijing like a loyal neighbor. Locals are proud of their history, Great Wall, and hearty dumplings. Visitors love the authentic atmosphere, friendly advice, and the way Langfang baked sesame cake shows up on every local menu.

Fun Facts:

• Langfang is home to the Grand Canal cultural area in Xianghe, a local highlight that reflects the city's character and history.

• The city blends everyday modern life with long-standing traditions, making it a great place to experience real China.

• Langfang baked sesame cake is one of the local flavors visitors remember most — ask a local where they eat it.

Speak Like a Local

Locals in Langfang have their own regional flavor of Mandarin. Try these to sound like one of the crowd:

中 (zhōng) — Good.

干啥 (gàn shá) — What are you doing?

得劲 (dé jìn) — Comfortable.

Famous People

• Zhao Yun — The legendary Three Kingdoms general was from Zhengding, near Shijiazhuang.

• Li Dazhao — A founder of the CCP was from Leting, near Tangshan.

• Chengde Emperors — The Qing emperors built their summer retreat in Chengde.
