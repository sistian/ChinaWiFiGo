Area & Population

Name Origin

Dalian (大连) means "Great Connection". Originally a small fishing village named Qingniwaqiao. A major port city and former Russian/Japanese concession with unique European architecture.

Happiness Index

7.2/10 — A seaside city with Russian soul — residents enjoy seafood, beaches, and European-style architecture in a Chinese setting.

Local Vibe & Fun Facts

Local Vibe: Dalian feels more like a European port city than a typical Northeastern Chinese city. It has squares, trams, hills, and a long coastline. Locals are direct, fashionable, and love seafood.

Fun Facts:

• Dalian was once a Russian port called 'Dalny' and later a Japanese city called 'Dairen.' Its architecture still shows both influences.

• The city is famous for its seafood, especially sea cucumber, abalone, and grilled squid.

• Dalian was once home to China's most successful football team, and locals are still passionate about the sport.

Speak Like a Local

Dalian locals speak Northeastern Mandarin with a coastal accent. Useful phrases:

嘎哈 (gá hà) — 'What are you doing?'

必须的 (bì xū dǐ) — 'Definitely / for sure.'

血受 (xiě shou) — 'Very comfortable / satisfying' — a Dalian slang phrase.

Famous People

• Bo Xilai — The controversial politician served as mayor of Dalian and is credited with beautifying the city in the 1990s.

• Footballers from Dalian Shide — Many Chinese national team players came from Dalian's golden football era.

• Dalian has produced naval engineers, shipbuilders, and seafood chefs who shaped China's northern maritime culture.
